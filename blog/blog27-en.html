How to Read Online Size Charts
— Flat vs Circumference

By Changyong Lee · FITME solo founder (South Korea)

Open the garment size chart before the “recommended size” table. Match shoulder, chest, and length (or waist, hip, rise, inseam for pants) to your notes within about ±1–2 cm. Letters and height/weight suggestions are secondary.

1. Why charts beat “recommended size”

I used to buy from height/weight suggestion tables on Musinsa and other shops: “175 cm / 78 kg → L.” The letter was often “right” and the garment still felt wrong — shoulders off, length long, pants waist OK but thighs failing.

Every brand grades L differently. Compare your measurement notes to listed garment specs. For intentional oversize failures, see the 2XL oversize guide.

Measure shoulders alone: shoulder width guide. No tape: hand-span measuring.

💡 Charts often say “±1–3 cm variance.” If you are on the edge, size for intentional ease or skip.

4. Flat vs circumference

Korean charts often list half-chest (garment laid flat). A “chest 55” is often ~110 cm circumference. US/EU charts more often list full circumference — never mix units.

5. 60-second checklist

  • Phone note: shoulder / chest / length (or waist / hip / rise / inseam)
  • Same unit as the chart (flat vs full)
  • Target within ±1–2 cm (or intentional oversize on shoulder only)
  • Height/weight “recommended size” is reference only
  • Unsure? Leave in cart and recheck tomorrow

6. Review search words

  • Tops: shoulder, seam, length, sleeve, boxy, tight
  • Pants: thigh, rise, inseam, gape, hip

7. FAQ

Is chest 55 cm circumference?

Often half-chest on KR charts. Roughly double for circumference.

I wear L — buy L?

Compare cm, not letters.

No chart?

Weak signals only — I usually skip no-spec oversize.
