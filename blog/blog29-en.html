1. Waist 32, thighs failed

I have bought jeans that fit the waist in the fitting room and failed the moment I sat down — thighs and crotch pulling. Standing mirrors lie.

After the waist letter, I check thigh, rise, hip. Related: WHR / jeans gap · pants silhouettes.

3. When to drop slim

  • Slim / skinny — little thigh room; high fail rate if thighs are muscular or full
  • Straight / tapered — my usual compromise: thigh ease, cleaner ankle
  • Wide / loose — less pinch; watch hem and shoe color (shorter-height notes)

4. Short-rise warning signs

  • Pull at the crotch when sitting
  • Belt digs uncomfortably
  • Tucked shirts look awkward at the hip

Low rise can feel trendy and still fail daily comfort. Mid/high rise sits better for me.

5. Checklist & review words

  • Compared thigh, rise, hip — not only waist
  • Sat down and stood up in the fitting room / at home
  • Tried straight/tapered before blindly sizing up
  • Scanned reviews for thigh / rise / sitting

Useful review words: thigh, rise, crotch, tight, sitting, stretch.

6. FAQ

Size up if thighs are tight?

Not always — waist may gape. Change silhouette first.

What is short rise?

Short waistband-to-crotch length; sitting discomfort is common.

Must I lose thigh fat?

Out of scope here — this is shopping fit, not diet advice.
